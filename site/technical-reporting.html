What the Report Should Make Clear

The final format should support both technical review and record control.

01

Content 1

Unique report and sample identification.

02

Content 2

Customer and site details.

03

Content 3

Transformer or equipment identification provided by the customer.

04

Content 4

Sampling information and sample condition as received, where applicable.

Content 5

Applicable notes, limitations and observations.
