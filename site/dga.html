Dissolved Gas Analysis

DGA - Detect. Diagnose. Decide Earlier.

DGA measures gases dissolved in transformer oil. The gas pattern and trend can support evaluation of electrical, thermal, insulation-related and air-related conditions when interpreted with asset history and operating data.

Diagnostic relevance

DGA Can Support Evaluation Of

The observed pattern may support further technical review of the following condition categories.

Partial Discharge

Low-energy electrical activity may influence the observed gas pattern.

Thermal Faults

Thermal activity and overheating can produce characteristic changes in dissolved gases.

Arcing

High-energy electrical discharge may require urgent technical review.

Overheating

Operating temperature, loading and localised heating should be considered.

Insulation Deterioration

Carbon oxides and companion evidence may support insulation review.

Air-Related Conditions

Oxygen and nitrogen data may support evaluation of ingress or sealing conditions.

Do Not Diagnose From One Number.

DGA interpretation must consider gas concentrations, relative patterns, rate of change, transformer type, operating history, previous results, loading and other available evidence. The website should not publish universal alarm limits or promise a diagnosis without the complete technical context.
