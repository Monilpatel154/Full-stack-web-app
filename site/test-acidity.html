Routine Test Knowledge

Acidity (Neutralisation Value) Testing.

Acidity is an indicator of oil oxidation and degradation. Rising acidity can be associated with ageing products and may precede sludge-related concerns. Trend comparison is important because one result alone may not explain the full asset condition.

Acidity (Neutralisation Value) explanation page

What It Assesses

Acidic oxidation products present in the oil.

Why It Matters

Acidity is an indicator of oil oxidation and degradation and is most useful when reviewed as a trend.

How to Use the Result

Review the stated method, units, sample condition, companion tests and historical trend before making a condition judgement.

Interpretation principles

Use the Result as Part of a Combined Technical Picture.

✓
Confirm the sample identity and sampling context.
✓
Check the applicable method, test conditions and units.
✓
Compare with earlier results where available.
✓
Review companion tests and transformer operating history.
✓
Escalate notable or unexpected changes for qualified engineering review.
