Official Laboratory Governance & Policy

LADLI Electrical Testing and Calibration Laboratory Private Limited (“LADLI”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of data privacy, client confidentiality and information security. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and safeguard information obtained through our website and testing services.

Information Collected

We may collect business contact details (name, company, phone number, email address), transformer asset details, testing requirements, uploaded technical specifications, and general website usage metrics necessary to deliver testing services and respond to inquiries.

Purpose of Processing

  • Respond to technical inquiries and provide structured quotations.
  • Coordinate oil sample submission, logistics and laboratory analysis.
  • Issue authorized laboratory test reports and diagnostic certificates.
  • Maintain quality, audit, regulatory compliance and business records.
  • Improve website usability, service performance and customer communication.

Confidentiality and Security

All customer information, test specifications and laboratory reports are treated as strictly confidential business records. We maintain disciplined access controls, secure digital infrastructure, and encrypted communications to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure or alteration.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses standard essential cookies and privacy-respecting analytics to optimize site performance and user experience.

Contact & Data Rights

For any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team at ladlielec@gmail.com or call +91 84908 38981.
