About LADLI

Science, Discipline and Reliability Behind Every Test.

LADLI Electrical Testing and Calibration Laboratory Pvt. Ltd. is a specialised laboratory focused on transformer insulating oil testing and diagnostic services. We help utilities, industries, EPC contractors and transformer manufacturers assess transformer health and reliability through scientific oil analysis.

Our story

Built to Make Transformer Oil Testing Dependable and Useful.

LADLI was established with a clear purpose: to make transformer oil testing dependable, understandable and useful for maintenance decision-making.

Our approach combines controlled sample handling, appropriate test methods, modern instruments, review and verification, and clear reporting. We are building a laboratory culture in which accuracy, integrity and customer responsibility are essential parts of every assignment.

Our commitment

More Than a Collection of Numbers.

Our commitment is to deliver precise test results, technical clarity and responsive service that support preventive maintenance, improve equipment reliability and help customers protect continuity of power.

We treat every report as a critical technical record that must be produced carefully and communicated clearly.

Vision

To be one of India's most trusted laboratories for transformer oil testing and diagnostic services.

Mission

  • Deliver precise and dependable testing.
  • Support transformer reliability and safety.
  • Provide timely reports with technical accuracy.
  • Follow applicable standard methods.
  • Build long-term relationships through quality and integrity.
